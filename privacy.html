PresaleRadar

Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026 · Last updated October 7, 2026

PresaleRadar is an Android app that finds concert presales for the artists you listen to. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. This page explains what the app reads, what leaves your phone, who receives it, how long it is kept and how to delete it.

  • Your music library and the screenshots you pick are read on your phone. Your files and images are never uploaded.
  • The app uses your home location, rounded to about 1.5 miles, to find concerts near you.
  • If you turn on presale alerts, that rounded location, your artist list and a notification token are stored on our server so we can notify you. Turning alerts off deletes them.
  • We don't sell your data, share it for advertising, or track you across other apps.

What the app reads on your phone

  • Your music library. With your permission, the app reads the names of folders and audio files in your Music and Download folders (including on an SD card), and in any folder you choose, to work out which artists you listen to. Only the resulting artist names are used, and your files stay on the phone. When a name comes from a file name such as "Artist - Song.mp3", the app checks just the artist part with Ticketmaster to confirm it is a real artist.
  • Screenshots you pick. When you import screenshots (for example, a list of your top artists), the app reads the text in them using Google ML Kit, which runs on your phone. The images and their text are not uploaded. ML Kit sends Google performance information, such as device model, Android version, image size and processing time.
  • Your location, only when you ask. If you tap "Use my current location", the app takes a single reading. There is no background tracking. The reading is rounded to about 1.5 miles and turned into a city name by your phone's built-in geocoding service, which Google provides on most Android phones. You can type a city or zip code instead.
  • Stored on your phone. Your settings, artist list and recent results are saved in the app's own storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Like most online services, our server host keeps standard technical logs, such as IP address and request time, for a short period to run and protect the service. We don't use them to identify or profile you.

Who else receives data

We use these service providers. Each handles data only as described above, under its own privacy policy.

  • Supabase hosts our database and server functions. Privacy policy
  • Ticketmaster provides concert and presale information and artist lookups. Privacy policy
  • SeatGeek provides additional concert listings. Privacy policy
  • Expo delivers notifications and app updates. Privacy policy
  • Google provides notification delivery (Firebase Cloud Messaging), on-device text recognition (ML Kit) and Android geocoding. Privacy policy

When you open a concert, the ticket seller's website opens in your browser, and its own privacy policy applies there. If you choose to share the app's debug report, it contains your artist list, city name and app diagnostics, and goes only where you send it.

We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Your choices

  • Turn alerts off in the app (menu, then Presale alerts). Your location, artist list, notification token and alert history are deleted from our server right away. The app keeps working on your phone, and turning alerts back on sends them again.
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your phone. If alerts were on, our server deletes your data the next time a notification can't be delivered, or at the latest 180 days after you last opened the app.
  • Change permissions any time in Android settings. The app still works without them: you can type artists in and enter a city instead of sharing your location.
  • Ask us. Because there are no accounts, we can't look up your data by name or email. Turning alerts off is the fastest way to delete it, but you are welcome to contact us with any question or request.

Children

PresaleRadar is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the app with alerts turned on, contact us and we will help remove the data.

Security

Data travels between the app and our server over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our database is closed to the public. Only our own server functions can read or change it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
